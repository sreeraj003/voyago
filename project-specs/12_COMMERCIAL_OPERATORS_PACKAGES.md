# 12 --- Commercial Operators and Paid Experiences

Introduce the commercial marketplace separately from free community
trips.

## Operator onboarding

-   business profile
-   owner account
-   business category
-   contact information
-   business address
-   verification documents
-   verification status
-   terms acceptance

Verification must be reviewed by authorised operations staff. Do not
assume that uploading a document proves legitimacy.

## Commercial package

Fields: - title - description - operator - destination - start/end
dates - price per participant - currency - capacity - inclusions -
exclusions - cancellation policy - meeting point - images - status

## Rules

-   Only approved operators can publish paid packages.
-   Clearly identify commercial listings.
-   Display total price, inclusions, exclusions and cancellation terms
    before booking.
-   Do not offer paid passenger transport or driver matching without
    specific legal review and required approvals.
-   Do not mix community trip expenses with commercial package payments.

## Operator dashboard

-   profile status
-   package management
-   bookings overview (read-only until booking milestone)
-   customer questions
-   performance summary

## Tests

Test verification gates, package publishing, price validation, capacity
and public display of required terms.

## Completion gate

Verified operators can create and publish commercial experiences, while
unverified operators cannot. Update docs.
